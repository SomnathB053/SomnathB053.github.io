I am an M.Tech student in Robotics at **IIT Jodhpur**, working at the intersection of robotic control, autonomous systems, and computer vision. Previously, I worked as a Junior ML Engineer at Rava.ai and interned at PwC India.

My current work focuses on multi-robot coordination, sim-to-real workflows, and high-frequency control loops for autonomous platforms. 

> 🎯 **Looking for Opportunities:** I am set to graduate in **mid-2027** and am actively seeking full-time roles in Robotics Engineering, Motion Planning, and Autonomous Systems.

### Research & Technical Interests

* **Aerial Robotics & Swarms:** Trajectory generation, multi-agent coordination, and flight testing with Crazyflie 2.1 & Qualisys MoCap.
* **Robot Dynamics & Control:** Implementation of controllers via `ros2_control`, physics simulations in MuJoCo & Gazebo.
* **Applied Perception & Learning:** Visual servoing, edge detection pipelines, and ML integration for real-time robotic tasks.
